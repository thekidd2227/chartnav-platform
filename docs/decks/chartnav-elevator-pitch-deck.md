> Under 5 minutes. 6 slides. Simple language. One clear CTA.

> intros, or the closing minutes of a longer meeting.

**Audience:** any first-touch listener — investor, advisor,
partner, or practice contact.

## Slide 2 — The 30-second story

- **Title:** What ChartNav does.
- **Content:**
  - Eye-care practices need more than a scribe. They need a
    workflow layer across **front desk → tech workup →
    imaging review → MD encounter → review / sign-off →
    checkout / follow-up / internal coordination.**
  - ChartNav ships **role-based clinic dashboards, structured
    retina + glaucoma tracking, an imaging metadata + review
    pipeline, the OD/OS retinal diagram with provider-reviewed
    annotations, and internal coordination**.
  - At the documentation layer, **Clinical Signal Filtering**
    does what scribes can't:
    **filters conversation, captures findings, builds the
    diagram** — separating casual speech from clinical
    findings, flagging uncertainty, and proposing retinal
    diagram annotations the provider applies, edits, or
    rejects.
  - Provider-reviewed at every step. Nothing finalizes without a
    click.
- **Speaker notes:** Read in under 30 seconds. Land "ophthalmology
  clinic workflow layer" out loud, then "filters / captures /
  builds" as the CSF cadence.
- **Visual:** 4-bullet card.

## Slide 3 — Why it's hard to copy

- **Title:** Why ophthalmology-specific.
- **Content:**
  - Built for eye-care lanes — front desk, technician workup,
    OCT / fundus / VF / biometry review, retina/glaucoma
    tracking, OD/OS documentation.
  - Clinical Signal Filtering tuned to how ophthalmology doctors
    actually talk.
  - OD/OS retinal canvas is first-class — not bolted on. Signed
    retinal artifacts are immutable; edits create an explicit
    fork.
- **Speaker notes:** This is the moat in three lines.
- **Visual:** 3-bullet card.

## Slide 4 — What ChartNav is not

- **Title:** Boundaries.
- **Content:**
  - Not a certified EHR replacement.
  - Not HIPAA-certified.
  - Not autonomous diagnosis.
  - Does not interpret OCTs, fundus photos, or visual fields.
  - Does not select IOL power or anti-VEGF dosing.
  - Does not autofill IOP, refraction, or cup-to-disc ratio.
  - Not a current vendor integration with Cirrus / Spectralis /
    Triton / Optos / IOLMaster / Humphrey / Topcon.
  - Not automatic orders / coding / referrals / patient
    messages.
- **Speaker notes:** Read aloud — boundaries earn trust quickly.
- **Visual:** plain bullets.

# ChartNav Elevator Pitch Deck

> Under 5 minutes. 6 slides. Simple language. One clear CTA.
> Designed for hallway conversations, ad-hoc investor or partner
> intros, or the closing minutes of a longer meeting.

**Audience:** any first-touch listener — investor, advisor,
partner, or practice contact.
**Purpose:** explain ChartNav in under 5 minutes and land one
clear next step.
**CTA / next step:** schedule a 5-minute fake-patient demo.

**Safe-claims contract.** Every slide obeys the approved-language
list at `docs/commercial/chartnav-approved-claims-language.md`.
ChartNav is provider-reviewed workflow support — no certified-EHR
replacement claim, no autonomous-diagnosis claim, no automatic
orders / coding / referrals / patient-messaging claim.

## Slide 1 — Cover

- **Title:** ChartNav.

- **Speaker notes:** If they only hear one line, this is it.
- **Visual:** logo + tagline.

## Slide 2 — The 30-second story

- **Title:** What ChartNav does.
- **Content:**
  - Ophthalmology doctors move fast — findings get buried in
    narrative notes.
  - ChartNav's **Clinical Signal Filtering** separates casual
    speech from clinical findings, flags uncertainty, and
    proposes retinal diagram annotations.
  - The provider applies, edits, or rejects every proposal —
    nothing finalizes without a click.
- **Speaker notes:** Read in under 30 seconds. Land "filters
  conversation, captures findings, builds the diagram" if you
  have time.
- **Visual:** 3-bullet card.

## Slide 3 — Why it's hard to copy

- **Title:** Why ophthalmology-specific.
- **Content:**
  - OD/OS retinal canvas is first-class — not bolted on.
  - Clinical Signal Filtering tuned to how ophthalmology doctors
    actually talk.
  - Signed retinal artifacts are immutable; edits create an
    explicit fork.
- **Speaker notes:** This is the moat in three lines.
- **Visual:** 3-bullet card.

## Slide 4 — What ChartNav is not

- **Title:** Boundaries.
- **Content:**
  - Not a certified EHR replacement.
  - Not autonomous diagnosis.
  - Not automatic orders / coding / referrals / patient messages.
- **Speaker notes:** Read aloud — boundaries earn trust quickly.
- **Visual:** plain bullets.

## Slide 5 — How it's offered

- **Title:** Demo first, then pilot.
- **Content:**
  - Live fake-patient demo first — no PHI, no risk.
  - Controlled pilot afterward, with BAA + security review.
  - Pricing discussed in the demo, not in the elevator.
- **Speaker notes:** Hand pricing only when asked. The elevator
  pitch sells the demo, not the contract.
- **Visual:** 2-step funnel.

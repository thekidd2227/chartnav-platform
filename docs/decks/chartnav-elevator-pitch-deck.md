> Designed for hallway conversations, ad-hoc investor or partner

## Slide 1 — Cover

- **Title:** ChartNav.
- **Content:** *"Provider-reviewed clinical workflow for
  ophthalmology."*
- **Speaker notes:** This is the one-line pitch. If they only hear
  one line, this is it.
- **Visual:** logo + tagline.

## Slide 2 — The 30-second story

- **Title:** What ChartNav does.
- **Content:**
  - Ophthalmology practices spend too much time on charts.
  - ChartNav surfaces a structured note, an OD/OS retinal canvas,
    a patient-friendly summary, and a pre-visit brief.
  - Every step is provider-reviewed. Nothing is automatic.
- **Speaker notes:** Read in under 30 seconds.
- **Visual:** 3-bullet card.

## Slide 3 — Why it's hard to copy

- **Title:** Why ophthalmology-specific.
- **Content:**
  - OD/OS retinal canvas is first-class.
  - Closed structured-note vocabulary tuned to ophthalmology.
  - Signed retinal artifacts are immutable; edits fork explicitly.
- **Speaker notes:** This is the moat in three lines.
- **Visual:** 3-bullet card.

## Slide 4 — What ChartNav is not

- **Title:** Boundaries.
- **Content:**
  - Not a certified EHR replacement.
  - Not autonomous diagnosis.
  - Not automatic orders / coding / referrals / patient messages.
- **Speaker notes:** Read aloud — boundaries earn trust quickly.
- **Visual:** plain bullets.

## Slide 5 — Pricing snapshot

- **Title:** Pricing.
- **Content:**
  - $299–$499 / provider / month, OR $5,000 / practice / month
    flat.
  - Pilot: $10,000 flat for a 4–6 week controlled pilot.
- **Speaker notes:** Don't open a long pricing conversation —
  hand the number, move to CTA.
- **Visual:** 2-line card.

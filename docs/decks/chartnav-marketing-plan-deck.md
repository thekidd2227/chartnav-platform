# ChartNav Marketing Plan / GTM Deck

> Internal go-to-market plan. 8 slides.

**Safe-claims contract.** Even internal marketing copy obeys the
approved-language list at
`docs/commercial/chartnav-approved-claims-language.md`. ChartNav
is provider-reviewed workflow support; marketing copy never
implies autonomous-diagnosis, automatic-orders, or HIPAA / SOC 2 /
FDA certification.

---

## Slide 1 — Cover

- **Title:** ChartNav — go-to-market plan.
- **Visual:** logo only.

## Slide 2 — Target buyer

- **Title:** Who buys ChartNav.
- **Content:**
  - **Primary:** ophthalmology practices (retina, glaucoma,
    cataract, general ophthalmology).
  - **Practice profile:** 2–10 providers, single-or-multi-location.
  - **Decision-makers:** clinical champion + practice administrator
    + security/compliance owner.
  - **Federal-healthcare adjacent:** SDVOSB-certified operating
    entity gives a path to VA-adjacent buyers; primary motion is
    private ophthalmology practice.
- **Speaker notes:** ChartNav is not a primary-care or
  specialty-agnostic play.
- **Visual:** persona card.

## Slide 3 — Channels

- **Title:** Where we find buyers.
- **Content:**
  - Direct outbound to ophthalmology practices.
  - Advisor / referral network (ARCG operating channels).
  - Public landing page (Phase 16) for self-discovery.
  - SDVOSB-channel federal healthcare opportunities (via the
    operating entity's existing past performance — Mann-Grandstaff
    VA).
- **Speaker notes:** Demo-first motion sits behind every channel.
- **Visual:** 4-bullet card.

## Slide 4 — Demo-first motion

- **Title:** Demos drive everything.
- **Content:**
  - Discovery call (15–30 min).
  - Live fake-patient demo (5 min) using Phase 15 Guided Demo Mode.
  - Hand-off the pilot readiness packet (Phase 14).
  - Decide to pursue or not pursue.
- **Speaker notes:** Refer to
  `docs/pilot/chartnav-demo-to-pilot-transition-plan.md`.
- **Visual:** 4-step funnel.

## Slide 5 — Pilot motion + pricing

- **Title:** From demo to controlled pilot.
- **Content:**
  - Pilot agreement.
  - Security review (BAA + readiness packet).
  - Controlled-pilot deploy (Postgres, `bearer` auth, backups,
    monitoring).
  - 4–6 week pilot.
  - Post-pilot decision.
  - **Pilot pricing:** $10,000 flat fee; pilot fees not discounted
    unless approved case-by-case.
- **Speaker notes:** Pilot fee is firm. Multi-practice discounts
  apply only to annual post-pilot agreements.
- **Visual:** timeline + pilot-fee card.

## Slide 7 — Referral / advisor strategy

- **Title:** Trust transfer.
- **Content:**

  - Practice-administrator networks.
  - Existing ARCG operating relationships (federal healthcare
    past performance opens federal-adjacent doors).
  - Demo-first motion applies to referrals too.
- **Speaker notes:** Don't pay for unverified referrals.
- **Visual:** 4-bullet card.

# ChartNav Marketing Plan / GTM Deck

> Internal go-to-market plan. 9 slides.

**Audience:** internal — operators, GTM advisors, partner
agencies producing co-branded outreach.
**Purpose:** lock the demo-first motion, the 30/60/90-day
execution plan, and the channels we use to find ophthalmology
buyers.
**CTA / next step:** execute the 30/60/90 plan; review at each
milestone gate.

**Safe-claims contract.** Even internal marketing copy obeys the
approved-language list at
`docs/commercial/chartnav-approved-claims-language.md`. ChartNav
is provider-reviewed workflow support; marketing copy never
implies autonomous-diagnosis, automatic-orders, or HIPAA / SOC 2
/ FDA certification.

---

## Slide 1 — Cover

- **Title:** ChartNav — go-to-market plan.
- **Visual:** logo only.

## Slide 2 — Target buyer

- **Title:** Who buys ChartNav.
- **Content:**
  - **Primary:** ophthalmology practices (retina, glaucoma,
    cataract, general ophthalmology).
  - **Practice profile:** 2–10 providers, single-or-multi-location.
  - **Decision-makers:** clinical champion + practice
    administrator + security / compliance owner.
  - **Federal-healthcare adjacent:** the SDVOSB-certified
    operating entity gives a path to VA-adjacent buyers; primary
    motion is private ophthalmology practice.
- **Speaker notes:** ChartNav is not a primary-care or
  specialty-agnostic play.
- **Visual:** persona card.

## Slide 3 — Channels

- **Title:** Where we find buyers.
- **Content:**
  - Direct outbound to ophthalmology practices.
  - Advisor / referral network (ARCG operating channels).
  - Public proof page for self-discovery.
  - SDVOSB-channel federal healthcare opportunities (via the
    operating entity's existing federal past performance at
    Mann-Grandstaff VA).
- **Speaker notes:** Demo-first motion sits behind every channel.
- **Visual:** 4-bullet card.

## Slide 4 — Clinical Signal Filtering as the wedge

- **Title:** Filters conversation. Captures findings. Builds the diagram.
- **Content:**
  - Clinical Signal Filtering is the easiest concept to explain
    in cold outreach: it answers "what does ChartNav actually
    do that a generic AI scribe doesn't?"
  - Use the three-line cadence in subject lines, opening calls,
    and demo cold-opens.
  - Pair with the ophthalmology-specific OD/OS canvas as the
    second visible differentiator.
- **Speaker notes:** Internal language — outreach copy still
  follows the brand-guidelines deck.
- **Visual:** four-row card showing the four classifications.

## Slide 5 — Demo-first motion

- **Title:** Demos drive everything.
- **Content:**
  - Discovery call (15–30 min).
  - Live fake-patient demo (5 min) using the buyer demo deck.
  - Hand-off the pilot readiness packet.
  - Decide to pursue or not pursue.
- **Speaker notes:** Skip the discovery call only when the
  practice is already pre-qualified.
- **Visual:** 4-step funnel.

## Slide 6 — Pilot motion + pricing

- **Title:** From demo to controlled pilot.
- **Content:**
  - Pilot agreement.
  - Security review (BAA + readiness packet).
  - Controlled-pilot deploy after gating items met.
  - 4–6 week pilot.
  - Post-pilot decision.
  - **Pilot pricing:** $10,000 flat fee; pilot fees not
    discounted unless approved case-by-case.
- **Speaker notes:** Pilot fee is firm. Multi-practice discounts
  apply only to annual post-pilot agreements.
- **Visual:** timeline + pilot-fee card.

## Slide 7 — 30/60/90-day execution plan

- **Title:** What we're doing right now.
- **Content:**
  - **Days 0–30 — pipeline build.**
    - Record ophthalmology-specific Clinical Signal Filtering
      demo clips.
    - Refresh the one-page sales leave-behind.
    - Build the discovery list (private practices + advisors).
    - Confirm 5 partner intro paths.
  - **Days 30–60 — discovery + pitch refinement.**
    - 10–20 ophthalmology discovery calls.
    - Capture every objection; update the objection-handling
      doc weekly.
    - Identify 3–5 pilot-candidate practices.
    - Refresh sales assets based on what's landing in calls.
  - **Days 60–90 — first paid pilot.**
    - Close the first paid pilot agreement.
    - Stand up the controlled-pilot environment for that
      practice.
    - Document the deployment process for the second pilot.
    - Update sales assets with first-pilot learnings.
- **Speaker notes:** This plan is operations work, not new
  product. We are deliberately not pulling forward clinical
  features.
- **Visual:** three-column 30/60/90 timeline.

## Slide 8 — Referral / advisor strategy

- **Title:** Trust transfer.
- **Content:**
  - Advisors with existing ophthalmology relationships.
  - Practice-administrator networks.
  - Existing ARCG operating relationships (federal healthcare
    past performance opens federal-adjacent doors).
  - Demo-first motion applies to referrals too.
- **Speaker notes:** Don't pay for unverified referrals. Partner
  economics conversations happen 1:1.
- **Visual:** 4-bullet card.

## Slide 9 — Metrics + milestones

- **Title:** What we measure.
- **Content:**
  - Discovery calls booked.
  - Live demos completed.
  - Pilot agreements signed.
  - Pilots completed.
  - Paid pilots — **target M1 = July 1, 2026; M2 = Oct 1, 2026.**
  - Paying customers — **target M3 = Q4 2026.**
  - Multi-practice deployments — **target M4 = Q4 2026.**
- **Speaker notes:** Real targets; we measure from zero. No
  fabricated baseline numbers.
- **Visual:** funnel + milestone table.

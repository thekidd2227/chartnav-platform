## Slide 3 — Pricing

- **Title:** ChartNav pricing.
- **Content:**
  - **Per-provider monthly subscription:** $299–$499 per provider
    per month.
  - **Pilot tier:** $10,000 flat for a 4–6 week controlled pilot.
  - **Per-practice flat tier:** $5,000 per practice per month
    (alternative to per-provider).
  - **Multi-practice annual discounts:** 2–4 practices = 10% off;
    5–9 practices = 15% off; 10+ practices = custom enterprise
    pricing.
  - **Pilot fees** are not discounted unless approved
    case-by-case.
- **Speaker notes:** This is the published pricing structure.
- **Visual:** pricing-tier table.

# ChartNav — 60-Second Call Opener (Phase 64)

> **Operator script for the first call with a prospect.** ~60
> seconds. Sets safe boundaries clearly. Asks 3 discovery
> questions. Avoids demoing anything ChartNav does not actually
> do.

## Pre-call checklist

- Open `docs/build/current-product-truth.md` in a side pane.
- Open `docs/demo/phase-61-buyer-qa-safe-answers.md` in another
  pane — that is the canonical Q&A bank.
- Confirm you have the prospect's practice name, role, and
  approximate practice size.
- Mute notifications.
- Do not promise compliance certification, revenue uplift, or time savings.
- Do not promise autonomous documentation; ChartNav does not generate notes without provider review.
- Do not promise ambient capture; ChartNav does not capture exam-room audio.
- Do not promise diagnosis support; ChartNav does not diagnose.

## Opener (~60 seconds, deliver verbatim or close to it)

"Hi <first name>, thanks for the time. I will keep this short.

I work on **ChartNav**, a **provider-reviewed workflow layer**
for ophthalmology and retina practices. It is not an EHR and does
not replace your EHR. It works alongside whatever you already
use.

We focus on four narrow things:

- structured **technician intake and vitals**;
- a **provider-reviewed VisitDraft** from a transcript the
  clinician types or pastes — not an ambient scribe, not exam-
  room audio capture;
- a **provider-reviewed Fundus Drawing Assist** from findings the
  clinician types — not image interpretation, not auto-grading;
- **doctor review, attestation, and signed lock** on every
  artefact.

We have a controlled fake-data demo (seeded fake patient, no real
PHI) that takes about 15 minutes. If the fit feels right we are
open to a short paid-pilot conversation, but only after a
security review before any real-PHI use.

Three quick questions if that is OK:"

## Discovery question set (ask 3 out of these; do not ask all)

1. "What does your current documentation workflow look like
   between technician, provider, and signed chart?"
2. "Where do you see the most handoff friction — intake, the
   draft note, imaging findings, or sign-off?"
3. "Have you tried any AI-assisted documentation tools before?
   If so, what worked or did not?"
4. "If we did a 15-minute fake-data demo, who else would you
   want in the room?"
5. "What would success look like in a 30 / 60 / 90-day
   controlled pilot — from your side?"

Listen more than you speak. Take notes into the outreach tracker.

## Hard-stop topics

If the prospect asks for any of these on the first call, do not
improvise:

| They ask for | You say |
|---|---|
| HIPAA-certified product or BAA today | "ChartNav is designed to support HIPAA-aware data-handling practices but is not HIPAA-certified. BAA discussions follow a security review before any real-PHI use. I can send the security-review packet index." |
| Ambient scribe / hands-free / exam-room audio capture | "ChartNav does not capture exam-room audio. VisitDraft Assist works from a transcript the clinician types or pastes. If a hands-free ambient scribe is the main need, we are likely not the right fit today." |
| Fundus image interpretation / OCT interpretation / auto-grading DR | "ChartNav does not interpret fundus photos or OCT images. The Fundus Drawing Assist takes clinician-entered findings text and produces a structured diagram. Image interpretation is not on the roadmap for the controlled demo." |
| Certified EHR / EHR replacement | "ChartNav is not a certified EHR and does not replace your EHR. It is a workflow layer that runs alongside." |
| Pricing on the first call | "Pricing is still a discovery topic for early controlled pilots. I would rather understand your workflow and constraints first, then come back with a pilot hypothesis." |
| Customer references / who else uses it | "ChartNav is in pre-pilot today. I do not have public customer references. Happy to share the safety frame, the demo, and the controlled pilot positioning instead." |

## Close (~15 seconds)

"Two next steps that usually make sense:

1. Send you the one-page brief and Q&A document.
2. Schedule the 15-minute fake-data demo for <next week>.

Which is more useful first?"

## Post-call (immediately)

- Update the outreach tracker
  (`docs/commercial/phase-64-outreach-tracker-schema.md`).
- Email the one-page brief if asked.
- Schedule the demo if asked.
- Do not commit to anything beyond the controlled demo without an
  explicit pilot conversation.

## Safety note

- Fake-data demo first.
- Paid pilot subject to security review for any real-PHI use.
- Provider review and sign-off required on every artefact.
- ChartNav is not HIPAA-certified.
- ChartNav is not a certified EHR and does not replace any EHR.
- ChartNav does not diagnose.
- ChartNav does not interpret fundus or OCT images.
- ChartNav does not place orders, send referrals, or message patients.
- ChartNav does not bill or code.
- ChartNav does not integrate with medical devices and does not provide remote patient monitoring.

## DM v1 — after connection accept

Hi <first name>, thanks for connecting.

Quick note in case it is useful: **ChartNav** is a
provider-reviewed workflow layer for ophthalmology / retina —
structured technician intake, provider-reviewed VisitDraft from
clinician-pasted transcript text, provider-reviewed Fundus
Drawing Assist from clinician-entered findings, and doctor
review / signed lock on each artefact.

It is not an EHR and does not replace your EHR. It does not
diagnose, does not interpret fundus or OCT images, does not place
orders, send referrals, message patients, bill, or code. It is
not HIPAA-certified — real-PHI use would only follow a security
review.

We have a 15-minute fake-data demo (seeded fake patient, no real
PHI). Worth a look?

— <your name>

## Operator notes (do not paste)

- LinkedIn DMs have a hard character limit; keep messages tight.
- Do not paste links to the public website in the first message;
  the website is a separate decision and Phase 64 does not edit
  it.
- Do not paste internal repo paths or screenshots in DMs.
- Do not claim other practices have signed pilots — none have.
- Do not promise time savings, revenue uplift, ROI, or clinical
  outcome improvements.
- After two messages with no reply, stop the cadence and mark
  the tracker status as "paused".

## Safety note

- Fake-data demo first.
- Paid pilot subject to security review for any real-PHI use.
- Provider review and sign-off required on every artefact.
- ChartNav is not HIPAA-certified.
- ChartNav is not a certified EHR and does not replace any EHR.
- ChartNav does not diagnose.
- ChartNav does not interpret fundus or OCT images.
- ChartNav does not place orders, send referrals, or message patients.
- ChartNav does not bill or code.
- ChartNav does not integrate with medical devices and does not provide remote patient monitoring.

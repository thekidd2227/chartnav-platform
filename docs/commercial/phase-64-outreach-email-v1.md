# ChartNav — Outreach Email v1 (Phase 64)

> **Cold / warm outreach to small-to-mid ophthalmology and retina
> practices.** Short. Documentation/workflow framing only. No
> hype, no compliance overclaim, no automation overclaim. Offer a
> controlled fake-data demo; mention a paid pilot only as
> exploratory.

## Subject line options

Use one. Keep it short. No exclamation points.

- "Quick note on the documentation workflow at <practice name>"
- "Provider-reviewed workflow layer for ophthalmology"
- "Controlled fake-data demo — 15 minutes"

## Email body

Hi <first name>,

I work on **ChartNav**, a provider-reviewed workflow layer for
ophthalmology and retina practices. ChartNav is not an EHR and
does not replace your EHR; it runs alongside your existing chart
system and focuses on four narrow workflows:

- structured technician intake and vitals;
- a provider-reviewed VisitDraft from a clinician-pasted
  transcript;
- a provider-reviewed Fundus Drawing Assist from clinician-
  entered findings text;
- doctor review, attestation, and signed lock on each artefact.

We have a controlled fake-data demo (seeded fake patient — no
real PHI) and can walk through it in about 15 minutes. If the fit
looks right, we would be open to a short paid-pilot conversation,
subject to a security review before any real-PHI use.

A few things ChartNav explicitly **does not** do today, because
practices ask: it does not diagnose, does not interpret fundus
photos or OCT images, does not place orders, does not send
referrals or patient messages, does not bill, does not code, and
is not HIPAA-certified.

Would a 15-minute fake-data demo be useful?

— <your name>
<your role>
<your contact>

---

## Operator notes (do not paste)

- Keep the email under ~180 words.
- Personalize the first line if you know one specific detail
  about the practice (specialty mix, location, a public talk).
- Do not promise revenue uplift, time savings, ROI, or clinical
  outcome improvements.
- Do not claim HIPAA compliance, certified-EHR status, autonomous
  documentation, or ambient capture. None of those are true.
- If the recipient asks for compliance documentation, route to
  `docs/commercial/phase-64-security-review-packet-index.md`.
- ChartNav does not provide an ambient / hands-free scribe; if the recipient asks for one, decline politely.

## Safety note

- Fake-data demo first.
- Paid pilot subject to security review for any real-PHI use.
- Provider review and sign-off required on every artefact.
- ChartNav is not HIPAA-certified.
- ChartNav is not a certified EHR and does not replace any EHR.
- ChartNav does not diagnose.
- ChartNav does not interpret fundus or OCT images.
- ChartNav does not place orders, send referrals, or message patients.
- ChartNav does not bill or code.
- ChartNav does not integrate with medical devices and does not provide remote patient monitoring.

# ChartNav — Follow-Up Email v1 (Phase 64)

> **Polite follow-up to the Phase 64 outreach email.** Short.
> Same safe positioning. No pressure tactics. No fabricated
> traction.

## When to send

- 7 to 10 business days after the initial outreach (Phase 64
  outreach email v1) if there is no reply.
- Never more than two follow-ups total before pausing outreach.

## Subject line options

Use one. Keep the thread.

- "Re: <original subject>"
- "Following up — 15 min fake-data demo"

## Email body

Hi <first name>,

Following up on my note from <date>. No pressure — we know
<practice name>'s inbox is busy.

The short version: **ChartNav** is a provider-reviewed workflow
layer for ophthalmology / retina. ChartNav is not an EHR and
does not replace your EHR; it supports structured technician intake, a
provider-reviewed VisitDraft from clinician-pasted transcript
text, a provider-reviewed Fundus Drawing Assist from clinician-
entered findings text, and doctor review and signed lock on each
artefact.

The demo runs against a seeded fake patient (no real PHI) and
takes about 15 minutes. If the fit is not right, no problem — I
appreciate you saying so.

If you do want to look, two options:

1. 15-minute fake-data demo this week or next.
2. A one-page brief I can send first if you would like to skim
   before scheduling.

— <your name>
<your role>
<your contact>

---

## Operator notes (do not paste)

- Keep the follow-up shorter than the original email.
- Do not introduce new claims that were not in the first email.
- Do not imply other practices have committed — none have.
- Do not invent a pilot price point. Pricing remains a discovery
  topic (see
  `docs/commercial/phase-64-paid-pilot-positioning.md`).
- ChartNav does not provide an ambient scribe, does not autonomously generate clinical documentation, does not interpret fundus images, and is not HIPAA-certified. If the recipient asks for any of these, decline politely and offer to send the safety-frame document instead.
- After the second follow-up with no reply: mark the tracker
  status as "paused" and stop the cadence.

## Safety note

- Fake-data demo first.
- Paid pilot subject to security review for any real-PHI use.
- Provider review and sign-off required on every artefact.
- ChartNav is not HIPAA-certified.
- ChartNav is not a certified EHR and does not replace any EHR.
- ChartNav does not diagnose.
- ChartNav does not interpret fundus or OCT images.
- ChartNav does not place orders, send referrals, or message patients.
- ChartNav does not bill or code.
- ChartNav does not integrate with medical devices and does not provide remote patient monitoring.

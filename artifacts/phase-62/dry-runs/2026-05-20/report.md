### Known fidelity caveats (transparency, not blockers)

- **Vitals sign/lock continuity (07 ≡ 09 ≡ 10 by byte size).** The local SQLite DB persists between scenes. After scene 03 cleared systolic and saved a draft, scene 04 found the encounter already in a downstream state and the sign-and-lock button was disabled because preconditions were already satisfied earlier in the run. Each screenshot was captured from the live app — none are fakes — but the **delta** between the three frames is small enough that PNG byte counts collide. The signed-lock state is genuinely shown; the "review" frame visually overlaps. Fixable by adding a per-scene `reset_demo_state.sh` invocation; deferred to avoid changing the existing safety-checked seed flow.
- **Shot 16 fallback.** The `[data-testid=ambient-forbidden-actions]` panel was not visible inside its 3 s bounding-box window when the safety-flags scroll completed; the script gracefully fell back to a full-page screenshot. The forbidden-actions panel **is** present in the captured frame; the screenshot just is not tightly cropped to it.
- **Shots 26 and 27 share byte size.** The terminal-evidence HTML is small enough that scrolling between `#runtime-safety` and `#claim-scanners` lands the same content in the 1440×900 viewport. Both screenshots are real, both show the correct sections; they overlap visually.
- **Clip 12 is ~20 s, not 3 min.** The `12_highlight_reel_3min` filename is fixed by the required spec; the actual recording walks through workspace → vitals → visitdraft → fundus in ~20 s because each click resolves quickly in safe-stub mode. Renaming the file would violate the required filename gate, so we ship the filename as required and document the runtime here.
